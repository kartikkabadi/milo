# The Thiel lens

A note on what Milo is copying from Greek identity, and what it is refusing to.

## The two lenses

**Marble, law, agora.** Innocent, light, real identities. Reading beats writing.
The agora is where people stand in the open and argue in daylight. Nothing is
hidden, nothing is performed, and the strongest argument wins rather than the
loudest voice.

**Torch and bronze.** Energy, but kept calm and lawful. A torch is light you
carry, not a fire you set. It is the thing that lets you keep working after
dark without becoming a raider.

Milo is built on both, and the combination is the whole differentiator: **a
calm, lawful watcher.** It is awake while you are not, it does exactly what it
was told, and it leaves a clean diff behind.

## What we took

| took | because |
|---|---|
| the light from the torch reference | it is warm, low, and directional — the opposite of a screen glow |
| the fluted column as terminal rhythm | vertical lines that are structure, not decoration |
| the Greek key as a divider and progress track | it is a signature that costs 2px |
| the laurel for success states only | a laurel that appears for anything else means nothing |
| the eyelid as a carved statue line | the sleeping face should look sculpted, not drawn |

## What we refused

| refused | why |
|---|---|
| war imagery | the UI is not a battlefield, and a watcher is not a soldier |
| the Spartan army aesthetic | we took the torch, not the phalanx |
| gold gradient text | gold is a key line and a success tick, nothing else |
| full marble photo backgrounds in the app | a photograph behind a diff is a diff nobody reads |
| costume fonts | no "Greek-looking" display face. The serif is a modern cut. |
| the Parthenon in the logo | the identity is geometry and light, not a building |
| above-the-law energy | this is a tool that asks permission, and it means it |
| night-raid energy | it commits a `wip(agent)` and leaves the diff. It does not delete your branch. |

## Why the distinction is load-bearing

Most agent tooling borrows from one of two registers: the **conqueror** (it will
do everything, it will replace you, it is inevitable) or the **hacker** (it is
clever, it is illegible, it is a little dangerous).

Milo is neither, and the Greek material is unusually well suited to the third
option. The agora is a place where you show your work in the open. Marble is a
material that records what was decided. The meander is a border — a thing whose
function is to say *this far and no further*.

That is the product. A watcher with a border. It wakes on CI fail, leaves a
diff, and goes back to sleep. It does not decide anything on your behalf, and it
does not pretend the decision was obvious.

## The one-line version

> Steal the light from the torch, not the army.

Everything in `brand.md` follows from that sentence.
